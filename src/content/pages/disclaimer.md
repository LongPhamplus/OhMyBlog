## Write-up policy

- I only publish write-ups for **retired** Hack The Box / TryHackMe content and for CTFs that have **ended**, following each platform's rules.
- Flags shown in write-ups are either from finished events or redacted.
- IP addresses, hostnames and credentials shown are lab values or have been redacted.

## Responsible disclosure

When I find a vulnerability in real software or services:

1. I report it privately to the vendor (security.txt, bug bounty program, or security contact) first.
2. I allow a reasonable fix window — by default **90 days** — before publishing, and coordinate the date with the vendor where possible.
3. If the vendor is unresponsive, I may escalate via a coordinator (e.g. a CERT such as VNCERT/CC for Vietnamese organizations) before any publication.
4. Published details are limited to what defenders need; weaponized exploits for unpatched issues are never published.
